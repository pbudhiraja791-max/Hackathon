Brand
The website name is Fluxora. Do not use EnergyX, EnerGrid, PowerPulse or any previous placeholder name.

Core promise

02 — PS08 PROBLEM
Invisible waste
The supplied concept identifies invisible energy waste caused by unmonitored HVAC, lighting and empty
facilities.
Passive monitoring
Legacy meters may only preserve historical information without telling an operator what to do next.
Maintenance
Equipment can continue operating in degraded conditions until failure, increasing energy and emergency
costs.
Renewable under-use
Solar, batteries and flexible loads can be operated without coordinated timing.
Design consequence
Fluxora must connect every major detection to a decision or action path.

Decision maker

Future market

16 — ANOMALY DETECTION
Model
Use Isolation Forest as the primary anomaly-detection approach described by the reference.
Targets
Spikes, unusual device behavior, equipment degradation patterns and phantom night loads.
Features
Power, time-of-day, rolling baseline, device category and contextual data.
Output
Score, severity, device/source, evidence window and recommended next step.
False positives

28 — OPTIMIZATION ENGINE
Goal
Minimize cost and/or peak demand while satisfying operating constraints.
Flexible loads
EV charging, battery dispatch, selected HVAC settings and other configurable loads.
Constraints
Availability, completion deadlines, battery SOC, charge/discharge limits, comfort and safety limits.
Method
Use linear programming or another explainable constrained optimizer.
Output
Feasible schedule, expected impact, constraints and recommended actions.

recommendations. 7. Recommendations can be approved. 8. Simulator/device layer responds to actions. 9. Post-action
